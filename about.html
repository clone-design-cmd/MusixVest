About MusixVest

Music royalties, opened up to fans

MusixVest connects everyday listeners with music royalty investing an asset class that's traditionally been limited to labels, publishers, and institutional funds. We're building it to be transparent from the first click, because we think that's the only version of this worth building.

Fans first

Anyone can start building a music royalty portfolio not just institutional funds and industry insiders.

Radical transparency

Every fee, every deposit, every distribution is visible in your dashboard not buried in fine print.

Artists keep creating

Rightsholders can raise funding directly from the people who already love their music.

What we're building

The platform lets artists and rightsholders list songs as SongShare offerings. Fans deposit funds, buy shares in a song, and track their portfolio from a personal dashboard. Every deposit is reviewed and confirmed by our team before funds are added to a fan's balance we explain exactly how and why on the How It Works page.

  • ✓ Fractional royalty investing through SongShares
  • ✓ Identity verification required before a fan can invest
  • ✓ Every deposit reviewed by our team for account security
  • ✓ A portfolio dashboard with full holdings and transaction history

Trust & compliance

How we handle your information and your money

Why we ask for identity information. Citizenship status, date of birth, and an ID number let us confirm you're eligible to invest and help prevent someone else from opening or accessing an account in your name. Your address should match the ID you'd use to verify in a full production system. We ask for what's needed to do that nothing more.

Security. Your connection to MusixVest is encrypted, and access to account data is restricted to authorized staff. We don't sell or share your personal information. Verification and account data exist to protect your access, not to be monetized.

Regulatory status. SongShares involve real money and real risk, and offerings like these are typically subject to securities regulation.
