About MusixVest

Connecting fans and artists through music royalties

MusixVest connects investors with music royalty opportunities, making it easier to discover catalogs and manage SongShares in one place.

What we're building

The platform lets artists and rightsholders list songs as "SongShares" offerings. Fans deposit funds, buy shares in a song, and track their portfolio from a personal dashboard. An admin reviews and confirms every deposit before funds are added to a fan's balance.

  • Fractional royalty investing through SongShares
  • Identity verification before a fan can invest
  • Admin-reviewed deposits for account security
  • A portfolio dashboard with holdings and transaction history
  • Meet & Greet access with artists you've invested in

Why we ask for identity information

Our verification flow is designed to protect account access and support a secure investing experience. Investors provide the information required to establish and maintain their account.
