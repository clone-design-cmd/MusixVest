The mechanics

How MusixVest works

From creating an account to owning your first SongShare here's the full path a fan takes on MusixVest, and exactly how money moves at each stage.

Identity verification required to invest Every deposit reviewed before funds are available Full history in your dashboard
  1. 1

    Create an account

    Register with your name, email, and a password. This gets you a MusixVest account, but you'll need to verify your identity before you can invest.

  2. 2

    Verify your identity

    Confirm you're eligible to invest by providing your date of birth, address, and identification details. This step exists to confirm you're old enough to invest and to keep your account from being accessed by anyone but you it's the same basic idea as identity checks on other investment platforms, just not outsourced to a third party yet.

  3. 3

    Deposit funds

    Choose a funding method bank transfer, debit card, or wire send your payment, and upload a screenshot of the confirmation. $200 minimum per deposit.

    Right now, a person reviews every deposit by hand before it's added to your available balance you'll see a "Pending review" status in your deposit history until it's confirmed. We're transparent about this because it means deposits aren't instant, and because we think you should know how your money is actually being handled.
  4. 4

    Buy SongShares

    Browse open offerings and buy fractional shares in the songs you want to back. Each offering lists a fixed price per share and how many remain the price doesn't fluctuate minute to minute the way a public stock does; it's set for the offering and holds until shares sell out.

  5. 5

    Track your portfolio

    Your dashboard shows your holdings, royalty distributions, and full transaction history every deposit, purchase, and payout, with nothing left off the record.

The mechanism

How royalty income reaches you

A SongShare isn't a copy of a song it's a fractional claim on the income that song generates. Here's the path that income takes.

1

A song earns income

Through streaming, downloads, sync licensing (film, TV, ads), and other uses of the song.

2

Royalty income is collected

Income tied to that specific song's rights flows in from the sources it was earned through.

3

Split by ownership

That income is divided across every SongShare in the offering, in proportion to how many shares each investor owns.

4

Shows up in your dashboard

Your share of a distribution appears as a transaction you can see, not a number that just changes silently.

Royalty income isn't guaranteed and varies by song some songs earn more than others, and some may earn very little. Past performance of any song is not a promise of future income. See the FAQ below for more on how risk works on MusixVest.
