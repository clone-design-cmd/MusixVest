How MusixVest works

From creating an account to owning your first SongShare, here's the full path a fan takes on MusixVest.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Register with your name, email, and a password.

  2. 2

    Verify your identity

    Confirm you're eligible to invest by providing your date of birth, address, and identification details.

  3. 3

    Deposit funds

    Request a deposit from your settings page. An admin reviews and confirms it before it's added to your balance.

  4. 4

    Buy SongShares

    Browse open offerings and buy fractional shares in the songs you want to back.

  5. 5

    Track your portfolio

    Your dashboard shows your holdings, royalty distributions, and transaction history.
