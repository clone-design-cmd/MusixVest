Top Questions

What are SongShares? ↓

SongShares represent real fractional ownership units in music royalty rights.

Do royalties come from Spotify, Apple Music, and other platforms? ↓

Yes, royalty collections stem from global digital streaming services, radio plays, and sync licensing.

Do I own the music rights? ↓

You own financial royalty rights linked to specified catalogs, rather than full copyright control over the master recording.

When do I receive royalty payments? ↓

Payouts occur on a scheduled basis, typically quarterly, directly into your account balance.
