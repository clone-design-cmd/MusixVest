Setting Up Your Account

To ensure you're eligible to purchase on SongTrade, we're required by U.S. regulations and the SEC to collect certain personal information. This helps us comply with identity verification requirements and keeps our platform secure.

Why We Need Your Personal Information?

  • User Type: Allows you to register as an individual or corporate entity.
  • Domicile: To comply with required laws around purchase, which may vary by jurisdiction.
  • Date of Birth: To verify you are eligible to invest.
  • SSN: If you have a SSN, we are required to capture this per SEC regulations. This data is stored in an encrypted format and can't be viewed by anyone at SongTrade.
  • Address: To help verify your identity and eligibility to invest. Make sure this matches the address on your current ID.
